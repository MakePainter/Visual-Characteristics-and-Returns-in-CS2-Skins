# Research context

This project is positioned as a small, transparent proof of concept rather than a replication of the broader literature.

- **Reichenbach (2025), “Skin in the game: The returns of digital assets from computer games,” Finance Research Letters 83, 107670.** Studies portfolios of more than 3,000 Counter-Strike cosmetic items and reports high historical returns alongside high volatility, transaction costs, and long-run uncertainty. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.frl.2025.107670
- **Pogorelova (2025), “Determinants of Counter-Strike Skin Prices: A Hedonic Pricing Approach.”** Uses transaction and image-derived characteristics in a pricing framework; useful context for treating visual features as potentially relevant but not dominant determinants. https://www.hse.ru/en/edu/vkr/1050416108
- **Taylor (2022), “An investigation on the pricing of virtual items & digital commodities.”** Provides earlier evidence on observable attributes and virtual-item pricing. DOI: https://doi.org/10.7273/000004977

The current results are compatible with a market in which visual attributes may matter while risk, liquidity, rarity, starting price, and other item characteristics remain important. The project does not claim that saturation is a universal CS2 pricing factor.
